About Shades Unisex Salon

Shades Unisex Salon is a small, owner-operated salon located in El Dorado, serving both male and female clients. With over 25 years of experience, the salon has built its identity around healthy hair care, reasonable prices and personal one-to-one service. The salon focuses on helping clients feel comfortable, confident and well cared for during each visit. Its friendly and professional approach allows customers to receive hair care that suits their needs while maintaining a personal connection with the business.
